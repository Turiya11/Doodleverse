How to Draw
  1. Draw: touch the board with your index finger, or pinch a crayon to pick it up and draw with its tip. Let go and it goes back to the tray.
  2. Neon crayon: the glowing purple one draws with light!
  3. Erase: pinch the eraser and rub it over a line. (Tapping a crayon or the eraser also chooses it for your finger.)
  4. Bring it to life: pinch the magic wand and wave it in a circle. Sparkles stream from the star and it sings louder the faster you wave. Or just tap the green Done button.
  • Too far to reach? Point at a crayon, the eraser or a button: a yellow ray appears. Pinch to choose it.
  • Move the board: pinch the grey handle under the tray and move your hand. The board turns to face you.
  • Lost? A small sign below your view always says what to do next, and an arrow points back to your board or your friend.
  • Exit: tap the red Exit button, then tap it again.
  • On a computer: click a crayon, drag on the board to draw, drag the grey handle to move it, and drag the wand in circles to bring it to life.

How to Play

Play fetch

  1. Pick up the ball: pinch it in the basket (or off the floor).
  2. Throw it anywhere: let go while your hand is moving. Your friend hops after it and catches it.
  3. Catch it back: when it throws the ball to you, bring both hands together where it's coming.

Missed it? The ball hops back into the basket by itself.
Tidy up: hold the ball over the basket until its rim glows green, then let go.

More

  • Carry your friend: pinch it with both hands and lift.
  • Make it jump: while carrying it, flick your hands up.
  • Walk around: explore your room safely.
  • Exit: point at the red floating diamond and pinch.
  • On a computer: drag the ball or your friend with the mouse and let go to throw; click the ball in the air to catch it; click the diamond to exit.
